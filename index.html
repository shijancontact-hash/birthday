Scene 01 / The beginning

The day you arrived

November 16, 2007. The world became special that day. A little star from heaven was born. She had no idea about all the places she would go and all the memories she would make. She didn't know who she would become and like how much she would grow and how many things she would learn or how much life would change her along the way. Sometimes I wish I could go back to that day even just for a moment and tell that little girl that she's going to grow into someone truly amazing. That life will bring her some of the happiest days and some difficult ones too ofc but that she'll make mistakes and learn from them and discover new things about herself. And most importantly I'd tell her that one day someone would look at her and think she's one of the most special people in their entire world. And that someone is me. I'd tell her that someone would fall in love with her laugh and her personality and the way she is and even the little things about herself that she probably doesn't even notice.

Scene 02 / Our story

Three years, and counting

It's a little sad that I wasn't there for those first years. I missed all those little versions of you(mini sheeny) all the memories you made before I even came into your life. And sometimes I really wish I could've known you back then just to see you grow up and experience all those little moments with you. But something that I find so crazy is that we've already been together for 3 years, and that's only around 15% of your entire life. Maybe to you that sounds like a long time, but to me, it feels like so little. Like I've known you forever, and somehow, three years still don't feel like enough. But then I think about it, and there's something beautiful about it too. Every single year, every experience, every person you've met and every little thing you've been through has helped shape the person you are today. And somehow, I get to know that person, love her and appreciate her in ways I hope she never doubts.
