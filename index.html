Scene 01 / The beginning

The day you arrived

November 16, 2007. The world became special that day. A little star from heaven was born (ahahahaha, yk aasti toilet ma huda I thought to start like this and I felt so proud that i came up to this but anyywayss). That little star had no idea about all the places she would go and all the memories she would make. She didn't know who she would become and like how much she would grow. Sometimes I wish I could go back to that day even just for a moment and tell that little girl that she's going to grow into someone truly amazing(dherai na maskiaa haiiii). That life will bring her some of the happiest days and some difficult ones too ofc but that she'll make mistakes and learn from them and discover new things about herself. And most importantly I'd tell her that one day someone would look at her and think she's one of the most special people in their entire world. And that someone is me. I'd tell her that someone would fall in love with her laugh and her personality and the way she is and even the little things about herself that she probably doesn't even notice.

November 16, 2007

Scene 02 / Our story

Three years, and counting

It's a little sad that I wasn't there for those first years. I missed all those little versions of you(mini sheeny) all the memories you made before I even came into your life. And sometimes I really wish I could've known you back then just to see you grow up and experience all those little moments with you. But something that I find so crazy is that we've already been together for 3 years, and that's only around 15% of your entire life. Maybe to you that sounds like a long time, but to me, it feels like so little. Like I've known you forever, and somehow, three years still don't feel like enough. But then I think about it, and there's something beautiful about it too. Every single year, every experience, every person you've met and every little thing you've been through has helped shape the person you are today. And somehow, I get to know that person, love her and appreciate her in ways I hope she never doubts.

Three years of us

Scene 03 / The details I keep

The little things

I think one of my favourite things about loving you is that there's just so much to love, and it's not always the big things either. It's all the little things that you probably don't even realise I notice. The way you laugh and the way you talk and your random expressions and the little things you say and your habits and all the silly things you do without even thinking about them. Even the smallest things somehow manage to make me smile.

I love how you can make an ordinary conversation feel like the best part of my day. How even when we're doing absolutely nothing, I still enjoy having you around. I love hearing about your day and listening to your random stories and just getting to know all the little details about you that make you you.

And I hope you never feel like you have to change any of those little things about yourself. Because I don't think you realise how special they are to me. Maybe they're just ordinary things to you, but to me they're a million little reasons why I keep falling for you.

My favourite little things

Scene 04 / Our future together

All the things we still have to do

Sometimes I think about how many things we still haven't done together and honestly it makes me so excited. We've already made so many memories and shared so many moments but there's still so much I want to experience with you. There's just so much more waiting for us.

I can't wait for the day when we're finally together and I don't have to miss you through a screen anymore. When I can actually hug you and hold your hand and annoy you in person (which I promise I'll do a lot), go on random little dates and just spend time with you without having to worry about when we'll have to say goodbye again.

I wanna travel with you and try new things and take a million pictures and have random adventures and even just do the most boring everyday things together. Because honestly, it doesn't really matter what we're doing as long as I'm doing it with you.

All our memories yet to come

Scene 05 / The everyday moments

The little moments together

I want to experience all the little things with you too. Waking up next to you and going out for no reason and laughing at the stupidest things and making memories out of days that don't seem special at all.

And I know not every day is going to be perfect but that's okay. I don't expect everything to always be easy. I just hope that no matter what life throws at us we just keep growing together and making the most out of everything.

Every little moment with you

Scene 06 / Watching you grow

Watching you grow

It's honestly crazy to think that I met you when you were 15 and now you're turning 19. Four whole years. When I think about it it makes me realise just how much time has passed and how much you've grown since then. I've seen different sides of you like watched you change and learn new things and experience different moments and slowly become the person you are today.

It's such a special feeling knowing that I've been able to witness so many different chapters of your life. You've grown so much and even though I know there's still so much waiting for you and I hope you know how proud I am of the person you're becoming.

You were 15 here...

Scene 07 / Your new chapter

Your new chapter

And now here you are 19 years old. I hope this new chapter brings you everything you've ever wished for and so much more. I hope I get to keep watching you grow and celebrating your achievements and being there for your little moments and making even more memories with you. And if there's one thing I know it's that I'm really lucky I got to meet you at 15 and even luckier that I still get to be here at 19.

Here's to everything ahead ❤️

Scene 09 / My biggest wish for you

My biggest wish for you

My biggest wish for you is that Mahadev always watches over you and guides you through every chapter of your life. I pray that Lord Shiva blesses you with good health and strength and peace and protects you from anything that could ever take away your smile. May he always guide you towards the things that are meant for you and give you the strength to get through the things that aren't easy.

And selfishly, I pray that Mahadev allows me to be beside you through many more birthdays and memories and chapters of your life. I hope I get to keep watching you grow and celebrating your happiness and making countless memories with you. May this new year of your life be filled with blessings and love and protection and everything beautiful that you deserve.

Har Har Mahadev.

May Mahadev always bless you ❤️
